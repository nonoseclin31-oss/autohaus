# Sample photographs — licences and credits

These four photographs illustrate the Big Toys starter listings. They are the
only images on this site that were not taken by Autohaus Motion, and they are
here so the section is not empty before the first real machine is photographed.
**Replace them with your own photographs as soon as you have them** — a
listing should show the actual machine for sale.

Every one is reusable commercially. Each was downsized to 1600 px wide for the
web; nothing else was changed. Where the licence requires it, the credit is
also printed at the end of the listing's own description, which is where a
visitor sees it.

## Why these and not a manufacturer's press photo

A press photo from a maker's media site is still that maker's copyright, and
using one to sell a machine is not what a press licence covers. In Germany
that is the kind of thing a rights holder answers with a cease-and-desist
letter and a bill. Everything above may be used commercially by anyone, which
is the only reason it is here.
